Evidence and accountability

We measure what we build, and publish how it performs.

Most infrastructure has no performance record after handover. We create one as a condition of doing the work, which is what makes a service contract, a warranty and an investment case possible at all.

Commissioning baseline

Flow, water quality, energy and output, measured on the day of handover and never reconstructed later.

Service log

Every visit and every fault, with response time and downtime hours recorded.

Published uptime

Performance against a 95% target, reported to the customer twice a year.

Household evidence

Hours returned, money kept, fuel and feed displaced. The numbers a household feels.

Why it matters. Nobody can regulate, finance or scale what nobody is measuring. Our service contracts generate that data site by site, as a by-product of being paid to keep systems running.

Where the evidence comes from

Nakifuuma, a working site and not a rendering.

A five-acre farm in Mukono, Uganda, operated by Regena and used as a shared coordination and learning centre. It is not a demonstration built to be shown. It is a working site with a resident host community.

A working demonstration

Visitors stand in it. It is not a slide.

A field data source

Real longitudinal numbers feed the work, not estimates.

A training ground

Methodology training includes a field day, not only a classroom.

A case study told with, not about

The host community's account, in their words, reviewed and signed off by them before anything is published.

Sector alignment

Where our practice meets the continental agenda.

Our model was built independently and maps directly onto the four pillars set for Africa's systems transformation in water and sanitation. Comparable alignments hold in energy access and the circular economy.

Pillar 01

Leadership

Convening, advocacy and the systems framing that connects delivery to policy, led by our partner CGSC.

Pillar 02

Regulation and accountability

Measured baselines and published uptime, the performance data rural regulation currently lacks.

Pillar 03

Professionalisation

Certified makers, trained operators and engineers formed on site.

Pillar 04

Strategic public finance

A viability test before construction, so financial credibility is proven and not assumed.

Reference: Africa Water Vision 2063 and the Kigali Communique on Systems Transformation.
