Nakifuuma, Mukono

A working farm, not a demonstration plot

Five acres where Regena runs its connected systems under the same rules it offers customers: a baseline on day one, a service log, and results published as they come in.

Somewhere to stand

Funders, customers and officials see it working before they decide.

A data source

Longitudinal numbers, not estimates.

A place to learn

Research, graduate associates and field days.

Told with the host community

Their account, reviewed and signed off by them before publication.

How we measure

Service measures and circular measures, published together

Service

Commissioning baseline

Flow, quality, energy and output on the day of handover.

Service log

Every visit and fault, with response time and downtime hours.

Published uptime

Against a 95% target, reported twice a year.

Circular

Waste recovered

Tonnes of manure and organic waste treated, not dumped or burned.

Fee offset

Share of the service cost paid by recovered-product income.

Household gain

Hours returned, money kept, fuel and feed displaced.

Why it matters. Nobody can finance, regulate or scale what nobody measures. The circular measures are what an investor or a circular economist needs to judge whether the model pays.

Where we fit

Built for Uganda’s move from a linear to a circular economy

FrameworkWhat it means for this work
Uganda NDP IV, 2025/26 to 2029/30Sets industrialisation and household income as priorities and includes promotion of the circular economy.
Uganda Circular Economy RoadmapA national process led by the Ministry of Trade, Industry and Cooperatives, backed by the African Development Bank’s Africa Circular Economy Facility.
Uganda Green Growth Development Strategy (2017)The national frame for low-carbon, resource-efficient growth that the roadmap builds on.
Africa Circular Economy Facility and AllianceThe African Development Bank’s dedicated trust fund for circular economy innovation, and the government-led alliance it supports.

Also contributes to Sustainable Development Goals 2, 6, 7, 12 and 13.

Sources: NDP IV (UNEP LEAP) · Uganda circular economy roadmap (The Independent) · Africa Circular Economy Facility (AfDB)
