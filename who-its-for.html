Who it’s for

Who it’s for

For funders, communities, students, environmentalists and circular economists: what Regena offers each, and the next step.

For donors, foundations and impact investors

Fund proof, see results, watch it pay back

We ask you to back the next system commissioned against a measured baseline, at a site you can visit. You receive the same reports our customers do.

What you fund

A defined proof point: one system, its baseline, its first year of service and its recovered-product income.

What you get back

Commissioning baseline, six-monthly uptime and fee-offset reports, and an open invitation to Nakifuuma.

How money is held

Through a written co-funding or service agreement with Regena Systems Africa Ltd, with audited accounts.

Who is accountable

Two named directors, and a viability test that stops projects that cannot pay their way.

Back a project

For communities, farms and enterprises

A system that pays part of its own way

Tell us what you need. We run the viability test with you, and we only build if a named operator, an affordable fee and a market for recovered products are in place.

What you get

Water, energy, sanitation or recovery sized to your site and your income, built as one system.

What it costs

A service fee agreed in writing, lowered by the sale of compost, feed or briquettes.

Who fixes it

Your trained operator first, Regena within 48 hours if it is beyond them.

What you can see

Your own uptime and fee-offset report, twice a year.

Ask for a viability check

For students, researchers and universities

Learn and research at a working site

Nakifuuma gives you real systems, real data and supervision from practising engineers, with a systems-science view from Prof. Amadei and CGSC.

Research topics

Resource recovery from livestock waste, disease barriers, solar productive use, circular unit economics.

Graduate associates

Paid early-career placements on Regena systems, with a clear appraisal pathway.

Open data

Anonymised site data for theses and papers, under a simple data-use agreement.

Field days

University groups can visit for practicals in water, energy and circular design.

Apply or propose research

For environmentalists and circular economists

What is recovered, what is avoided, what it earns

We publish what our systems keep out of rivers, landfills and the air, and whether the recovered products actually pay. Both matter, or the model does not scale.

Recovered

Manure and organic waste turned into compost, protein feed and briquettes, measured in tonnes.

Avoided

No tree cutting for energy, no fired bricks, less untreated waste reaching water.

Earned

Recovered-product income as a share of the service cost: the number that decides scale.

Kept safe

Disease barriers built into every recovery step, so reuse does not pass harm on.
