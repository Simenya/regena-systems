About

Systems thinking, community engineering, and the discipline to stay.

Real progress rarely arrives as an external fix. It emerges from communities themselves, and it only lasts if someone is paid and accountable for keeping it running. That is the problem Regena was built to work on.

Who we are

A community engineering company.

Regena Systems Africa Ltd designs, builds, commissions and maintains affordable water, sanitation, clean energy, resource recovery, shelter and food systems for the places that need them.

Mission

To help communities, governments and allied organisations co-create integrated, participatory, systems-aware solutions to complex development and peacebuilding challenges.

Solutions are defined, owned and led by the communities themselves, and built to still be working when anyone checks back.

Vision, the next 15 years

Resilient, resourceful and just communities that solve their own challenges and live in harmony with people and planet.

Communities able to sustain livelihoods, adapt to ecological and political risks and cultivate peace, through collective intelligence, long-term vision and regenerative systems. Development that emerges from within and is not imposed from outside.

Operating principles

The 4Rs: what makes a solution succeed.

R1

The right solutions

Clarity of purpose and minimum adverse impact on people and ecosystems.

R2

Rightly done

Co-created with communities in ways that honour their knowledge, culture and lived experience.

R3

For the right reasons

Driven by ethical intent and long-term thinking, not short-term expediency.

R4

With the right mindset

Respectful of context and scale, and of what each community actually needs.

A systems-based approach

Infrastructure that works is also stability infrastructure.

Water scarcity, land pressure, energy poverty and climate variability move people and strain communities. We do not claim to build peace. We claim that systems which keep working remove some of the pressures that make life unstable, and we measure it alongside everything else.

Human development

Conditions that let people realise their full potential.

Human security

Localised risks to well-being and stability, identified and addressed.

Peacebuilding

Inclusive, systems-aware processes that address root causes and strengthen social cohesion.

The 5Ps, integrated: People, Planet, Prosperity, Partnership and Peace. Built into every project, with trade-offs surfaced rather than buried, and grounded in local decision-making and human rights.
