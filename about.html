About Regena

Why we exist

Mission

To design, build and operate connected water, energy, sanitation and food systems in which recovered waste pays part of the cost of the service, proven at a working site before it is scaled.

Vision

African communities and enterprises where nothing useful is wasted and essential services pay their own way.

Operating principles

Our operating principles: the 4Rs

PrincipleMeaning
R1The right solutionsLeast harm to people and ecosystems.
R2Rightly doneWith communities, honouring their knowledge, culture and lived experience.
R3For the right reasonsEthical intent and long-term thinking, not short-term expediency.
R4With the right mindsetRespectful of context and scale, and of what each community needs.

The 4Rs draw on the work of Prof. Bernard Amadei.

Leadership

Two directors, one standard

BA

Prof. Bernard Amadei

Director and co-owner

  • Distinguished Emeritus Professor of Civil Engineering, University of Colorado Boulder
  • Founding president of Engineers Without Borders-USA and co-founder of the EWB-International network
  • Founding director of the Mortenson Center at CU Boulder
  • Elected member, US National Academy of Engineering and National Academy of Construction; former US Science Envoy
  • At Regena: systems design review, research partnerships and mentoring; leads partner Common Ground Solutions Center
PN

Peter Nzabanita Sabiiti

Founder, Managing Director and co-owner

  • Civil engineer (BSc, Ndejje University), trained in water engineering from diploma level
  • MSc Water and Sanitation Engineering, Kyambogo University (conferment December 2026)
  • Research: resource recovery from cattle and pig waste, with barriers to disease transmission
  • In water and sanitation since 2004, including World Vision and IOM
  • At Regena: runs delivery, Nakifuuma and the commercial model
