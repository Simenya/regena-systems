Why Regena

We stay until it works.

Water, sanitation, energy and food projects are usually funded and delivered separately. Each one ends at handover, and few have a performance record after that. Together they rarely add up to a working system.

Regena builds them as one system and stays under a paid service contract to keep them running. Waste from one becomes the input to another, and that income is what keeps the service affordable.

What we build

Six systems, one practice.

See everything we build

Water

Boreholes, solar pumping, rainwater storage and water quality.

Sanitation

Toilets, handwashing, faecal sludge handling and safe reuse.

Renewable energy

Solar generation, productive-use energy and backup, with no tree cutting.

Resource recovery

Organic waste turned into compost, protein feed and fuel briquettes.

Shelter

Food systems

Irrigation, livestock water, soil health and farm infrastructure.

In partnership

Regena proves it.

We deliver alongside Common Ground Solutions Center, LLC, a United States consultancy with two decades of systems practice in engineering for development. They model and plan. We build, commission and maintain. Two companies, with separate contracts and separate accountability.
