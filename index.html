The idea

Waste from one system pays for the next

Most water, energy and food projects are funded one at a time, and each one ends up with a running cost nobody can carry. We build them as one system. Manure and organic waste are treated safely, turned into products people buy, and that income lowers the fee for the service.

Nothing useful is wasted. Nothing harmful is passed on.

  1. Manure and organic waste
  2. Treated safely, disease barriers
  3. Compost, feed, briquettes
  4. Sales income
  5. Lower fees for water and energy
  6. More food and livestock output

The whole loop is one system.

What we build

Six systems, one circular practice

See everything we build

Resource recovery

Manure and organic waste into compost, protein feed and fuel briquettes, with barriers to disease.

Water for productive use

Solar pumping, rainwater storage, irrigation and livestock water, with quality tested.

Renewable energy

Solar power for pumping, processing and cold storage. No tree cutting.

Sanitation and safe reuse

Toilets and handwashing where sludge is treated and returned to the soil safely.

Food systems

Soil health, irrigation and farm infrastructure that let farms grow, store and sell more.

Low-carbon building

Interlocking blocks pressed from local subsoil. No firing, no firewood.

Resource recovery leads because it is what makes the rest affordable.
