How the partnership works

Who leads at each stage.

StageLed by
Community diagnostics and baselineCGSC
Systems modelling and scenario planningCGSC
Engineering design and specificationJoint
Viability test: can this fund its own upkeep?Regena, final say
Construction and commissioning against a measured baselineRegena
Paid maintenance and uptime reportingRegena
Evidence, publication and feedback into the modelJoint

The viability test is deliberately Regena's alone. A systems model can recommend an intervention that is correct on every axis except one: who pays to keep it running. That gate keeps the partnership's evidence honest rather than merely elegant.

CGSC methodology

A ten-stage, systems-aware process.

Used to help decision-makers and practitioners develop community-scale scenarios. Later stages feed back into earlier ones as understanding deepens.

  1. 01

    Situation space

    Pre-appraisal, identification

  2. 02

    Community baseline

    Appraisal, participatory research

  3. 03

    Problem space

    Key issues, hypotheses

  4. 04

    Landscape dynamics

    Root causes and relationships

  5. 05

    Modelling dynamics

    Scenarios and structures

  6. 06

    Alternative scenarios

    Rank and select

  7. 07

    Decisions and theory of change

    Leverage and impact

  8. 08

    Intervention strategy

    Design, framework, M&E

  9. 09

    Implementation and exit

    Deliver and hand over

  10. 10

    Reflective practice

    Learn and feed forward
