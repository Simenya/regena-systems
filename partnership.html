Partnership

Two companies, two countries, one systems practice

The two companies

Who does what

Common Ground Solutions Center and Regena work together but stay separate companies, with separate contracts and separate accountability.

United States

Common Ground Solutions Center, LLC

Where
Lafayette, Colorado
Focus
Engineering for peacebuilding and human security
Work
System dynamics modelling and scenario planning; a ten-stage, systems-aware community diagnostic
Reach
Governments, foundations and universities
Limit
Does not build, install or operate infrastructure.

Uganda

Regena Systems Africa Ltd

Where
Kampala and Nakifuuma, Mukono
Focus
Circular-economy engineering: waste into income
Work
Design, build, operate and measure: resource recovery, water, energy, sanitation, food, building
Reach
Paid service contracts with published results
Limit
Has the final say on whether a system can pay for its own upkeep.

We present together because the work is complementary. We are not, and do not intend to become, one entity.

How the partnership works

Who leads at each stage

Seven stages run from the first community diagnostic to published results. Each has a named lead, so it is always clear who is accountable.

1Community diagnostics and baselineCGSC
2Systems modelling and scenario planningCGSC
3Engineering design and specificationJoint
4Viability test: can it pay for its own upkeep?Regena, final say
5Construction and commissioning against a baselineRegena
6Operation, maintenance and recovered-product salesRegena
7Evidence, publication and feedback into the modelJoint
CGSC leadsJointRegena leads

Why the viability test is Regena’s alone. A model can recommend an answer that is right on every count except one: who pays to keep it running. That gate keeps the partnership’s evidence honest, not just elegant.
